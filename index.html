Jason - The Street Racer

Jason is a former street racer who gets pulled into the criminal underworld of Vice City. Known for his quick reflexes and fearless attitude, he becomes the protagonist of an unexpected journey through the city's darkest corners.

Special Abilities: Expert driver, quick reflexes, street smarts

Vehicles: High-performance sports cars and custom rides

×

Lucia - The Strategist

Lucia is a brilliant strategist with a mysterious past. She partners with Jason and brings intelligence and cunning to their operations. Her mastery of hacking and planning makes her an invaluable ally.

Special Abilities: Hacking, planning, infiltration

Expertise: Tech systems, social engineering

×

Marcus - The Crime Lord

Marcus is a ruthless crime boss who controls much of Vice City's underworld. With connections everywhere and resources beyond measure, he becomes a central figure in the game's narrative, offering both opportunities and threats.

Special Abilities: Criminal connections, intimidation

Resources: Money, weapons, safe houses
